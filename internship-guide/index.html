Summer 2027 in Utah, for a 3D artist

Internships, part-time jobs and portfolio-builders near home for a first-year RIT 3D Digital Design student, sorted from long shots to realistic bets.

Read this first: most big-studio art internships target juniors and seniors, so apply to them as low-cost long shots. The theater, planetarium and camp jobs further down are where a first-year student has the best real odds. Several applications open this fall, so the timeline below matters more than the order of the list.

Game studios

Long shots for a first-year student, but applying costs nothing and gets your name in front of recruiters.

Avalanche Software (WB Games)

Salt Lake City
  • Paid
  • 11-week summer program
  • Some roles want rising juniors+

The Hogwarts Legacy studio and Utah’s biggest game employer. It has a history of art interns across concept, animation, rigging, FX, UI, environment, character modeling and storyboarding.

Warner Bros. Discovery’s tech and games internship track opens around mid-September with rolling offers by December, so the summer 2027 window is open now. No Avalanche-specific art posting had appeared yet, so check regularly.

careers.wbd.com (search “intern” and “Salt Lake City”)

Epic Games

Salt Lake City studio, plus other offices
  • 2027 internships posting now
  • Portfolio can stand in for experience

Epic is already listing 2027 internships with flexible start dates. One example is a Tech Art intern for Rocket League, though that one is in North Carolina. It explicitly accepts a strong enough portfolio in place of the usual program requirement.

Epic’s Salt Lake City studio has hired artists for technical art, animation, rigging, character, hard-surface, texture and VFX roles, so watch for intern listings there.

epicgames.com/earlycareers

Smaller Utah studios

Orem and Salt Lake City
  • No formal program
  • Best odds via cold email

Small studios sometimes take on a motivated student even without a posted internship. Send a short email with a portfolio link and a specific offer of help (props, environment pieces, texturing).

Wahoo Studios (Orem) is an independent console and PC developer with about 11–50 people (wahoo.com). WildWorks (Salt Lake City) makes games for kids and families. Strange Reptile (Salt Lake City) is an indie studio founded by industry veterans.

Planetariums and immersive media

A niche where Blender and real-time skills are used every day.

Clark Planetarium

Salt Lake City
  • May – August
  • Posted in late March last year
  • Blender-heavy production team

Clark ran a summer intern position through Salt Lake County from May to August 2026. Its production manager is known in the planetarium world for using Blender to make dome shows, so it’s worth asking directly whether any production help is possible.

Salt Lake County careers (last year’s posting, for reference)

Cosm (formerly Evans & Sutherland)

Salt Lake City
  • Company-wide summer program
  • Juniors and seniors only

Cosm builds dome theaters and immersive content and runs a summer internship that includes its Salt Lake City office. Listings require junior or senior standing, so save this one for a future summer.

cosm.com/careers

Theater props and scenic shops

Paid, hands-on 3D work: sculpting, fabrication, painting and finishing. Great portfolio material and a real résumé line.

Utah Shakespeare Festival

Cedar City
  • Paid seasonal
  • Stage crew ~$15–17/hr
  • Postings appear in October

A Tony-winning repertory company with a summer and fall season. Its prop shop staff listing for summer 2024 went up in late October 2023, so the 2027 version could appear any day. Look for prop shop, scenic and stage crew roles.

Southern Utah University job board

Tuacahn Center for the Arts

Ivins, near St. George
  • Paid internships
  • Scenic, paint and props departments

An outdoor Broadway-style theater producing several big musicals a season. It offers paid internships for college students across all departments, including scenic, paint and props.

tuacahn.org/internships

Haunted attractions

Wasatch Front
  • Summer build season
  • Prop and set building

Utah’s big haunts (Nightmare on 13th, Fear Factory, Castle of Chaos and others) build sets and props before the Halloween season, and some build year-round in permanent buildings. A spring email with photos of sculpting, props or 3D-printed pieces could land a build-crew spot.

Teaching at summer camps

The most realistic paid option for a first-year student, and it keeps your software skills sharp.

iD Tech at the University of Utah

Salt Lake City
  • Paid seasonal
  • Hires in winter and spring
  • Teaches 3D modeling and game design

iD Tech added a University of Utah location in 2026. Its course catalog includes 3D modeling and printing, game design and VR, and it hires a large number of instructors every summer.

idtech.com/jobs/summer-jobs

U of U Youth Education camps

Salt Lake City
  • Paid per contact hour
  • Game, animation and 3D camps

Instructors develop and teach week-long summer camps. Several game camps (3D worlds, game animation, Maya modeling) are co-sponsored with the U’s top-ranked games program and are often taught by its faculty and grad students, so an assistant role may be the easiest way in. Reach out in January.

Conditional options

Good fits only in specific circumstances.

Church magazines design internship

Salt Lake City
  • Paid, 20 hrs/week
  • May – August
  • Membership required

The Youth Digital Designer internship runs four months at the Church Office Building and welcomes animation and video work in portfolios. Applicants must be members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and currently temple worthy. Last cycle’s deadline was early March.

Apply through Employment on ChurchofJesusChrist.org.

Utah Jazz and Utah Mammoth creative

Sandy
  • Values Blender, C4D, After Effects
  • Current roles run August to August

The teams’ creative services department hires production and video interns who know motion graphics. Current listings are year-long, so this only works if a summer-only role appears.

Free ways to build a portfolio and network

This is how people hear about small-studio and contract work that never gets posted.

Utah game dev community

Statewide and online

The Utah Indie Games group holds game nights every other month and a game jam each fall (meetup.com/utah-indie-games). The Utah Games Guild runs a Slack for local developers (utahgamesguild.com), and there’s a Salt Lake chapter of the IGDA with regular meetups.

Show up with a project to share. Local artists and studio staff attend these.

RIT career services

Remote, start now
  • Avg co-op wage ~$22/hr
  • Unpaid work can count

Co-ops are encouraged in the 3D Digital Design BFA, and unpaid work may qualify for co-op credit with department approval, which makes smaller gigs more worthwhile. The program’s career services contact is Gretchen Eshleman, Associate Director (585-475-5462). RIT’s Handshake board is worth filtering by Utah, too.
