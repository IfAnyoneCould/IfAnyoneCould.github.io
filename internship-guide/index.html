Internships, gigs & programs for a first-year 3D artist

This list covers game studios, animation and VFX studios, and jobs outside those that use 3D art: toys, theme parks, museums and AR. It also covers contests, scholarships and ways to earn money from your art. Each entry is tagged by how realistic it is for you right now.

The short version
Watch the graduation-date filter

Most big game studio internships (Activision, Nintendo, Insomniac) require you to graduate between 2027 and 2028. They screen by graduation date, not class year, so for now read the eligibility line on every posting.

Some studios do take freshmen

DreamWorks and Walt Disney Animation take any enrolled student. Riot and Epic don’t list a class-year minimum. Hallmark, NASA and the Smithsonian take rising sophomores or younger students.

Creative Industry Days is the best first step

RIT runs free 1:1 portfolio reviews with industry artists on Jan 20–22, 2027. Sign up through Career Connect by searching “CID”.

Build your portfolio with credited work

Game jams, Rookies contests, MAGIC Spell Studios projects, Roblox UGC items and assets sold on Fab. All of these count, and they get you ready for sophomore-year applications.

Portfolio

What studios want to see

  1. An ArtStation portfolio with 8–15 of your best pieces, not everything you’ve made. Group it by discipline: 3D props and environments, characters, 2D concept.
  2. Breakdowns for 3D work: turntables, wireframes, texture maps, and Marmoset or Unreal renders. Riot’s guide says class work, jam work and fan art are fine as long as they’re yours.
  3. Show fundamentals for 2D and vis dev: perspective, composition, color and lighting. DreamWorks names these directly.
  4. A 60–90 second reel, only if you’re applying for animation, VFX or tech art. Put your strongest work first and your name on screen.
  5. A PDF version of the portfolio, because some applications (such as WB) ask for one.
Checked and ruled out, so you don’t have to
  • Naughty Dog, Valve and Bungie don’t have art internships right now (Bungie has had none since 2023). Naughty Dog hires interns through Sony/PlayStation.
  • Walt Disney Imagineering internships usually require junior year or above. The Disney Imaginations competition is the realistic route in for now.
  • The Unreal Fellowship requires 3+ years of professional experience.
  • Limit Break Mentorship is UK and Ireland only.
  • Microsoft Explore is a CS and product rotation, not an art path.
  • Women in Animation mentorship circles are for final-year students and working professionals, but their scholarships are open to you.
  • NICKternship is juniors and up. Framestore Launchpad is final-year students only. NFL Films 3D motion is grad students only.
  • Penguin Random House’s art internship requires full-time availability by June 2028 and closes Oct 2, 2026.
  • The Funcom and Table Knight internships are closed. AMNH MEEP is for NYC residents only.
  • The IGDA Next Gen Leaders and Velocity programs are for mid-career professionals.
